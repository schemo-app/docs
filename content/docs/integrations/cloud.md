Connect to a Schemo Cloud server when you want to sync a workspace and share it with a team. You need an account with access to that server.

## Connect a workspace

Open **Settings → Account**, set the server address, and start sign-in. Complete sign-in in the browser and return to Schemo. Use the workspace menu to open a shared workspace or link a local workspace, depending on your account access.

Collections, environments, and history can sync in linked workspaces. The workspace role determines the changes you can make. See [Local data]({{< relref "/support/local-data" >}}) for how local and synced data are handled.

## Publish a collection

Open the collection's sharing controls and choose **Publish**. Preview the page with its requests and examples, choose public, unlisted, or password-protected access, and publish it. Copy the resulting URL for your readers. You can review or take down published pages from **Publications**.
